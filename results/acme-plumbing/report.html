Automated Invoice Generation on Job Completion

about 29 h a month now saves about 17 h a month moderate to set up medium risk

What happens today

First one of 8 people (Ben, Ryan and others) does 'complete job in jobs'; then Emma does 'create invoice in accounts'. This happens about 292 times a month.

What starts it

A field worker marks a job as complete in the jobs system.

Steps the automation takes

  • Detect when a job status changes to complete in the jobs system.
  • Extract job details, customer information, and line items from the completed job.
  • Draft a new invoice in the accounts system using the extracted details.
  • Send the drafted invoice to Emma for review and approval.

Where AI helps

  • Extracts job details and line items to automatically draft the correct invoice in the accounts system.

Where a person still decides

  • Emma must review and approve the draft invoice in the accounts system before it is sent to the customer.

Why this risk level

Incorrect invoice amounts or details could lead to billing errors and customer dissatisfaction.

How the hours were worked out

292 times a month × 6 minutes = 29.1 hours. An automation like this usually removes about 60% of that work.
